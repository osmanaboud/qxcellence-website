THE FOUNDER

Experience that connects quality to business performance.

Osman Aboud is a senior Quality, Operational Excellence and Strategic Transformation executive with more than 25 years of professional experience across major infrastructure, construction, aviation, data centre and government-related programmes.

Throughout his career, he has held senior quality leadership positions, helping organisations strengthen enterprise quality governance, improve project performance, enhance compliance and assurance, and embed sustainable continuous improvement. His experience includes complex programmes such as Diriyah, King Khalid International Airport, NHC, Red Sea developments and data centre projects in Saudi Arabia.

His leadership approach combines strategic direction with practical delivery experience. He believes quality must be integrated into business strategy, project governance, risk management, supply chain performance and operational decision-making — rather than treated simply as an inspection or compliance activity.

Osman is also a strong advocate of Quality 4.0, artificial intelligence, digital assurance, data analytics and predictive quality management. He is particularly interested in how emerging technologies can transform traditional quality systems into proactive, intelligent and measurable business performance capabilities.

"Quality is not just about meeting requirements. It is about creating confidence, improving performance and delivering sustainable business value."

Through QXcellence, Osman brings together executive advisory, professional development, organisational transformation and digital innovation to help organisations and professionals achieve higher levels of performance and business excellence.
