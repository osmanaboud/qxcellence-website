ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Experience that connects quality to business performance.

Osman Aboud is a senior Quality and Strategic Transformation Leader with more than 25 years of professional experience across major construction, infrastructure and government-related programmes, with extensive experience in Saudi Arabia and international project environments.

Throughout his career, Osman has held senior leadership positions with responsibility for quality governance, enterprise assurance, integrated management systems, operational excellence, compliance, audit and performance improvement. His experience spans complex infrastructure, aviation, building, utilities and data centre environments.

His professional approach goes beyond traditional inspection and compliance. He believes quality should be embedded in organisational strategy, project governance, leadership accountability and everyday decision-making.

Osman has contributed to major programmes and projects including Diriyah, King Khalid International Airport, National Housing Company developments, Red Sea projects and data centre programmes.

"Quality is not simply about finding defects. It is about building the leadership, systems, intelligence and culture that prevent problems, improve performance and create lasting value."

As Founder and Principal Advisor of QXcellence, Osman brings this experience to organisations seeking stronger quality systems, improved project delivery, digital assurance and sustainable business excellence.
